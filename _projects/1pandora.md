## Overview
Pandora was an internal secrets management application used by 10,000+ Ubisoft employees across North America and Europe.

## My Role
Frontend Engineer. I worked on React/TypeScript UI implementation, reusable interface patterns, and cross-functional collaboration with design, product, and backend partners.

## Problem
Teams needed a clearer and safer way to store, retrieve, and share sensitive credentials across internal workflows.

## Contribution
- Built UI flows for viewing, creating, and managing different secret types
- Helped implement permission-aware sharing interfaces
- Collaborated with backend engineers on data shape, authorization behavior, and edge cases
- Worked with design/product partners to make complex security workflows easier to understand

## Design / Engineering Challenges
- Making permission states understandable
- Supporting multiple secret types without fragmenting the UI
- Balancing security constraints with usability
- Keeping frontend patterns maintainable as the product grew

## Outcome
Pandora supported secure credential workflows across a large internal employee base and became part of Ubisoft’s internal platform tooling.
